# Case Study: Transforming Enterprise Retail Execution via AI-Powered Shelf Auditing

**Industry:** Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

**Project:** U-lens (Enterprise Retail Intelligence Platform)

**Focus Area:** Computer Vision, Field Force Automation & Trade Marketing Compliance

---

### **Executive Summary**

For enterprise FMCG manufacturers operating in dense, fragmented retail environments, maintaining shelf visibility and trade marketing compliance is a critical revenue driver. Traditional retail auditing relies on manual field reports, creating blind spots, delayed decision-making, and unverified promotional execution across hundreds of thousands of retail touchpoints.

**U-lens** was engineered as an enterprise-grade retail intelligence platform designed to eliminate audit lag and revenue leakage. By combining on-device and cloud computer vision with automated field force management, U-lens converts simple smartphone photos into real-time, verified shelf intelligence—streamlining field operations, enforcing planogram compliance, and automating performance payouts.

---

### **The Challenge: The Retail Blind Spot**

Prior to deploying automated retail intelligence, managing field execution across vast general-trade networks presented several operational bottlenecks:

* **Manual & Biased Auditing:** Field auditors manually recorded shelf presence, Share of Shelf (SOS), and Point of Sale Materials (POSM) on paper or static forms, leading to high error rates and subjective reporting.
* **Delayed Visibility:** HQ teams faced weeks of latency between on-the-ground execution and consolidated performance analytics, preventing real-time corrective actions.
* **Unverified Trade Execution:** High investments in branded displays, shelf talkers, and planograms were difficult to verify at scale, resulting in misplaced or missing promotional assets.
* **Payroll & Incentive Disputes:** Calculating performance-based incentives manually for large field teams created administrative overhead and frequent disputes over execution accuracy.

---

### **The Solution: U-lens Architecture**

U-lens delivers an end-to-end ecosystem connecting field force agents, regional supervisors, and central brand management through three unified software layers:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         U-LENS PLATFORM                               |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+
|  1. Merchandiser Mobile App       |  • 1-Second AI Shelf Scan         |
|                                   |  • Dynamic Route & PJP Navigation |
|                                   |  • Real-time On-site Feedback     |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+
|  2. Supervisor Management App     |  • Live GPS Check-ins & Routes    |
|                                   |  • AI Challenge Mechanism         |
|                                   |  • Joint-Call & Coaching Logs     |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+
|  3. Central Executive Portal      |  • Territory & User Hierarchy     |
|                                   |  • AI Model Retraining Pipeline   |
|                                   |  • Automated Incentive Payroll    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+

```

#### **1. AI-Driven Shelf & POSM Intelligence**

Field agents open the application, select their scheduled Permanent Journey Plan (PJP) route, and snap a single photo of the retail shelf.

* **Multi-SKU Recognition:** Advanced computer vision models detect and segment hundreds of individual SKUs simultaneously, calculating Share of Shelf (SOS) percentages in real time.
* **POSM Verification:** Automated detection checks for the presence, condition, and correct placement of brand display items (standees, shelf talkers, danglers).
* **Exclusivity Rules:** The system automatically flags competitor encroachments into restricted or contracted display zones.

#### **2. Supervisor Quality Control & AI Feedback Loop**

* **GPS Check-in Validation:** Ensures agents perform audits physically inside assigned stores using verified location tags and photo proof.
* **AI Challenge Mechanism:** Supervisors can review "Success/Failed" automated audits and submit manual challenge logs with detailed feedback, feeding directly into continuous model retraining.

#### **3. Centralized Command Center & Payroll Automation**

* **Live Coverage Analytics:** Central dashboards display real-time territorial coverage, store-by-store audit histories, and compliance heatmaps.
* **Automated Incentive Payroll:** Performance metrics auto-calculate based solely on verified execution data, removing manual inputs and eliminating month-end payout friction.

---

### **Key Features & Technical Highlights**

* **Sub-Second Inferencing:** Fast image processing generates instant visual bounding boxes and share-of-shelf charts directly on the agent's screen.
* **Offline-First Capabilities:** Field agents can capture and log data in low-connectivity rural zones, auto-syncing seamlessly once back online.
* **Dynamic Journey Planning:** Administrators can re-route daily targets, assign dynamic schedules, and push immediate planogram updates to thousands of devices instantly.
* **Standardized Data Hygiene:** Centralized data pipelines ensure uniform data formatting (e.g., standardized lowercase email constraints and unified SKU naming conventions) across all exportable business intelligence reports.
